# Story — Corallum

## Premise
For four hundred years the **Long Silt**, a slow grey snowfall of ground stone, has buried every
reef in the sea. In this world a reef is memory: each polyp lays down a thin calcite band of what
it witnessed, so the buried reefs are drowned archives. The player is the first living polyp of a
new reef, settled on a seamount 4,000 m down.

**End goal:** breach the surface on the night of the Spawning Moon and release the reef's spawn,
carrying the archive into a new sea. The surface is visible from the first minute as a faint
silver ripple far above.

## Cast and places
- **Ossel** — narrator; the last living polyp of a dead reef wall nearby. Kind, certain, and
  wrong about several things it remembers.
- **Thousandfold** — the old reef-city that remembered everything and never stopped growing.
- **The Rasp** — beaked grazers that grind dead coral to sand (modelled on real parrotfish).
  Thousandfold walled them out; they are the reef's gardeners, not its enemies.
- **The Long Silt** — the catastrophe; it is Thousandfold's own skeleton, ground down.
- **The Spawning Moon** — the night the whole reef spawns at once.

## Arc
1. *The Silt Floor* — Ossel teaches you to grow. First bands read: Thousandfold's glory.
2. *The Midnight Water* — lanternfish and glass shrimp. Bands start to contradict Ossel.
3. *The Twilight Shelf* — the currents; the Rasp return. The truce. The truth about the Silt.
4. *The Sunlit Crown* — colour. Choosing a dialect: what kind of reef you will be.
5. *The Breach* — the Spawning Moon. You choose which memories the spawn carries; the
   epilogue follows from that choice. Theme: a reef that keeps everything calcifies and dies.

### Act II beats (Midnight Water)
- The lanternfish shoals remember the reefs only as old songs; they come because of the glow.
- Readings from Thousandfold's buried bands: its glory, its walls, the "beaked ones" it shut
  out, its currents slowing, its last bands thick with fear.
- Bands start contradicting Ossel. Ossel insists Thousandfold fell to the Rasp.
- End of Act II: a Reading shows the wall that kept the Rasp out, from the inside. Ossel's
  voice is in it. Ossel was part of that wall.
